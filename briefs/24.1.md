You are the builder for batch 24.1 of Anamnesis (Python 3.12, uv). Anamnesis is a pre-visit medication-intake tool: it reports where a patient's spoken account and the photos of their medicine packs disagree, and it never gives advice. Keep each change surgical: no refactors, and nothing outside this brief.

## Part 2: the brief (`anamnesis/brief.py`, `anamnesis/brief_html.py`, `anamnesis/schema/templates.py`)
5. **One codes line per finding box.** It goes under the two sides when the finding's claim `name_text` resolves to a catalogue entry with codes, or, for a finding with no claim, when its pack's `label_text` does. The line reads `Catalogue codes: ATC C07AB07 · RxNorm 19484`:
   - only the codes the entry has (an entry may carry one without the other);
   - several RxCUIs joined with ", " ("RxNorm 161, 1886");
   - every code escaped, and no links.
6. **One reference line per page,** after the last section, only when any codes line was shown: `ATC codes: WHO Collaborating Centre for Drug Statistics Methodology, as listed by AEMPS CIMA on 29 Oct 2026. RxNorm codes: RxNav. Sources and terms: NOTICE.` A brief with no code has no reference line. Don't name NLM in the brief: its terms ask apps not to use its name, and its required statement is in `NOTICE`.
7. **Both strings live in `templates.py`,** like every user-visible string. `_finding_html` takes the catalogue from `render_brief`; keep every other caller working.
