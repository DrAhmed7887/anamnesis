## The change: `_STYLE` in `anamnesis/brief_html.py`, plus one tag
The clinician brief is the product's main screen, and it reads like a plain document: browser-default indents, blue underlined links, nothing separating one finding from the next. Restyle it as a calm, modern clinical document that stays just as legible printed on A4 and on a phone.

**Two changes, and nothing else:**
1. **The `_STYLE` string** in `anamnesis/brief_html.py`.
2. **One viewport tag** in the page head, which `anamnesis/brief.py` writes (line 121). Put `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1">` right after `<meta charset="utf-8">`. Without it a phone lays the page out 980 px wide and shrinks it, so the 640 px rule never applies.

No other line in `anamnesis/` changes: no new class, id, attribute, tag, word or script line. The contract renders five briefs, removes their style and that one tag, and compares hashes taken before this batch, so any other change fails it.

### The page as it is: only these hooks exist
- **`header#scope`:** an `h1`, four `p` scope lines, then `p#review-status`. The status is one of "Draft: not yet reviewed by a clinician.", "Waiting for the patient: new photos requested 2." or "Reviewed and approved by the clinician."
- **Eight `section`s,** each with an `h2`, in this order: `#disagree`, `#across`, `#unconfirmed`, `#agree`, `#stopped`, `#coverage`, `#photos`, `#voice`. An empty one holds `<p>None.</p>`.
- **A finding** is `article[id^="finding:"][data-type]`, in this order:
  - an `h3[dir=auto]`, the medicine's name;
  - a `p` with the finding's sentence, and often a second `p` with the question for the patient;
  - sometimes `p[data-request]`, "A new photo has been requested from the patient.";
  - `div.sides`, holding `div[data-side="patient"]` and `div[data-side="pack"]`. Each has an `h4` ("Patient account", "Pack photo"), then a `dl` of `dt`/`dd` rows or a `p` ("Not mentioned.", "Not photographed."). Some `dd` are `dir="auto"`, amounts sit in `bdi[dir=ltr]`, and label text in `span.pre`;
  - sometimes a `p` "Catalogue codes: ATC … · RxNorm …";
  - always last, the evidence `p`: "Heard in: `<a href="#turn:…">`…`</a>`; Seen in: `<a href="#region:…">`…`</a>`".
- **`#across`:** `article[data-kind]`, with `p` lines and a `ul` of `li > a[dir=auto]`.
- **`#coverage`:** a `ul` of `li`.
- **`#photos`:** `figure[id^="photo:"] > div.photo > img + svg > a > rect` (the boxes on the photo), then `figure[id^="region:"]` with an `img` (the crop) and a `figcaption`.
- **`#voice`:** `audio[controls]`, then a `ul` of `li[id^="turn:"]`. Each turn has a `button` ("▶ 0:04–0:09") and a `span[dir=auto]` transcript, and clicking it plays that turn.
- **After the sections:** sometimes a `p` reference line ("ATC codes: WHO Collaborating Centre…"), then `details#timeline`, collapsed, with its `summary` "How it got here" and a 9-column `table` (`thead`, `tbody`, `tfoot`).

Selectors such as `article > p:last-child`, `.sides ~ p`, `body > p` and `:has()` are fine.

### What it should look like
- **Type:** `system-ui, sans-serif`, about 16 px, line height about 1.5, with a clear heading scale. Use tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) for amounts, times and the timeline.
- **Colour:** a white page, near-black text, one muted grey for secondary text.
- **Findings as cards:** a hairline border, a small radius, comfortable padding and space between cards. In `#disagree`, each card carries the amber accent `#d9480f` on its leading edge (`border-inline-start`), in place of today's bar on the whole section.
- **The two sides:** two columns divided by a hairline, each headed by its `h4` as a small muted label. Each `dl` is a two-column grid, label then value, with no browser indent.
- **The header:** the scope lines as quiet secondary text. `#review-status` is a small neutral pill. It must not look like an approval stamp: the same style serves every status.
- **Links:** one calm link colour. The evidence links become small bordered chips. Links in running text stay recognisable without colour.
- **Voice turns:** each row is clearly clickable (pointer, a light hover background), and the time button is quiet.
- **Timeline:** a light header row, striped body rows, numbers right-aligned as now, and a bold total row.

### Hard rules
1. **Nothing loads:** no `url(`, `@import`, `@font-face` or web-font names. System fonts only.
2. **No words from CSS:** a `content` value is only ever `""` or `none`, so no emoji or icons from CSS either.
3. **Nothing hidden:** no `display: none`, `visibility: hidden`, `opacity`, `font-size: 0` or `color: transparent`. Every word on the page stays visible, in print too.
4. **Keep the photo box rules and the image rule as they are:** `.photo svg rect{fill:transparent;stroke:#d9480f;stroke-width:3;vector-effect:non-scaling-stroke}`, `img{max-width:100%;height:auto}`, and the `.photo`, `.photo img` and `.photo svg` positioning. So each box stays on its pack, the whole box stays clickable, and no image is wider than its column.
   - `tests/unit/test_brief_extra.py` pins these as text. It also pins today's bar on the whole section, `#disagree{border-left:` (line 252).
   - Replace that one assertion with one that pins the accent's new place on each card. Change nothing else in that file, and no other existing test.
5. **Layout:**
   - The two sides sit side by side, stack below 640 px, and are side by side again in print (`@media print`).
   - At 375 px nothing makes the page wider than the screen. The timeline table may scroll sideways inside `#timeline` (`overflow-x: auto`); the page never does. Long ids and words wrap (`overflow-wrap: anywhere`).
6. **Contrast:** WCAG AA for every text colour on its background: at least 4.5:1, or 3:1 for large text (24 px, or 18.7 px bold). Borders and the accent that carry meaning are at least 3:1.
7. **Calm:**
   - no red;
   - no translucency, blur, `backdrop-filter` or gradients;
   - no animation or transitions;
   - no `position: fixed` or `sticky`;
   - no dark-mode block (light only);
   - shadows, if any, barely visible.
8. **Print:** a white background and no shadows. A card never splits across pages (`break-inside: avoid`), and the two sides stay side by side.
9. **Compact:** one rule per line in the string, as now, and about 60 rules at most.
