# Ledger integrity and retries (B12a: QM M2 #8 blocker and #4, M2 F-07)

You are the builder for batch 12 of Anamnesis (Python 3.12, uv). The quality manager put the project on HOLD because calls that may have been billed aren't always on the spend ledger: a timeout, for example, is never charged. Voice and SAM also have no retry, despite SPEC §4.

## Goal
Make `tests/contract/test_b12_ledger.py` pass, keeping all other tests green. No network in tests.

## Rules to implement
1. **Billing-uncertain failures are charged at the pre-call estimate.** That covers a timeout, a transport error after sending, any 5xx, and a 200 whose body is malformed (missing `id`, `usage`, `output`, etc.). The estimate is the same value passed to `guard.check(...)`:
   - Voice: the cost of the clip's duration
   - SAM: $0.0025
   - Spark: `SPARK_CALL_ESTIMATE_USD`
2. **Rejected requests are never charged:** 4xx and 429.
3. **One retry** for Voice, SAM, Spark labels and claim extraction, on retryable errors: timeout, transport error, 429, 5xx, a malformed body, and (Spark only) invalid JSON. `guard.check` runs before **each** attempt, so a cap reached after attempt 1 raises `SpendCapExceeded` before attempt 2 is sent.
4. **Every attempt stays visible.** `ProviderTrace` gains `prior_call_ids: list[str] = []` and `prior_cost_usd: Decimal = Decimal(0)` (≥ 0). The trace returned after a retry is the final attempt's, carrying the earlier attempts' call ids (real, or a generated `estimated-<uuid>` for charged failures) and their total cost.
5. **Ledger lines:** add a JSON boolean `estimated` to every line, and `reason` to estimated lines (for example `"timeout"` or `"http 503"`).
   - Add `SpendGuard.charge_estimate(model_id, amount, reason) -> str`, which returns the generated call id.
   - Estimated lines count toward `total_usd` and the cap.

## Where
- `anamnesis/providers/base.py`: `ProviderTrace` fields, and a retry helper that collects prior attempts.
- `anamnesis/providers/spend.py`: `charge_estimate`, plus the `estimated` and `reason` fields.
- `anamnesis/providers/meta.py`: `MetaClient.post` must expose enough to tell billable-uncertain failures from rejected ones (for example a flag on `ProviderError`, or a dedicated subclass). Wrap Voice, SAM and Spark in the shared retry.
- `anamnesis/providers/claims.py`: the same retry and charging.
- `SPEC.md` §5: one line on the ledger rule. Your own unit tests in `tests/unit/test_ledger_extra.py`.
