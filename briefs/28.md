# A second label reader for a pack the first still can't read after its zoom (SPEC §4 step 5b)

You are the builder for batch 28 of Anamnesis. Read SPEC.md §4 step 5b, the §4 "Budget" line, §4 "The second reader in the live script (batch 28)" and §8's `add_photo` lines first: they are the specification. The contract tests are `tests/contract/test_b28_second_reader.py`.

## Why
- **The main miss in the live columns is reading, not reasoning.** On 30 Oct, Spark 1.3 read four of the eight staged packs as partial in at least one run, even after its 2× zoom. Muse Spark Open on Fireworks, reading the same crops, read three of them correctly.
- **The loop already escalates once:** a partial reading gets one `zoom_read` (`anamnesis/agent/loop.py`, `_zoom`). Batch 28 adds one more step, a second model's read, before the pack falls back to `unreadable_source` and the clinician's re-photo request.
- **Nothing changes without a second reader:** no step, no call, the same budget and the same report.

## Build
1. **The action:** `AgentAction.SECOND_READ = "second_read"` (`anamnesis/schema/report.py`), after `ZOOM_READ`.
2. **The step** (`anamnesis/agent/loop.py`): `run_intake` gains a keyword `second_reader=None`. A helper beside `_zoom` takes the reading after the zoom and, with a second reader and a reading still `unreadable` or `partial`, makes one `second_read` call on `crop_region(image, region)` (the first read's pixels, not enlarged) with `cropped=True`, then applies SPEC §4 step 5b: the region check first, then readable, then the ingredient and strength check. It runs for each region right after that region's zoom.
3. **The budget:** the default is `2 + (len(concepts) + 3 × top_k) × photos` only when a second reader is given; otherwise it stays as it is. An explicit `max_provider_calls` is used as given.
4. **The re-photo** (`anamnesis/review.py`): `add_photo` gains `second_reader=None` and reads the new photo's abstaining readings the same way. Its default budget is `len(concepts) + 3 × top_k` only with a second reader.
5. **The live script** (`scripts/intake_live.py`): `--second-reader none|fireworks` (default `none`), a `Run.second_client` field, the second reader built as the SPEC says, the guards before any key or client, the budget, and `second_reader` in each `runs.jsonl` line. Update the docstring.
6. **Your own tests** go in `tests/unit/test_b28_extra.py`.
