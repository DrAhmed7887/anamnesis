# A salt said inside a longer label (SPEC §3, batch 33)

You are the builder for batch 33 of Anamnesis. Read SPEC.md §3, "Decision order for one claim and one pack", rule 6, with its new bullet "A salt said inside a longer label (batch 33)" and the two bullets after it: they are the specification. The contract tests are `tests/contract/test_b33_label_salts.py`.

## Why
Salts for each side come from one shared path, `_side_salts` in `anamnesis/core/reconcile.py`. It reads `name_text` and `label_text` through `_text_salts` (whole-word curated aliases, and `_loose_salts`), but each strength ingredient's and intake's `inn` label only through `part.salt or salt_of(part.inn)`, an exact alias. So a salt said inside a longer label is never read: "tomo metoprolol succinate" or "tomometoprolol succinate", as an intake or a strength label, is a `match` against a Metoprolol tartrate pack on `main`. That hides a formulation difference from the clinician.

## Build
1. **In `_side_salts`:** also read each strength ingredient's `inn` and the intake's `inn` the way the side's free text is read, through `_text_salts`, beside `part.salt or salt_of(part.inn)`. It applies to both sides, since packs share the path.
2. **Nothing else changes:** `_nominal_pack_amount`'s own salt reading, the scalar `inn` (`salt_of`), `INN_ALIASES` and every other salt table, `Catalogue._matches`, the claim parser and the schema's validator all stay as they are. Add no salt name.
3. **Your own tests** go in `tests/unit/test_b33_extra.py`.

## Hard rules
1. **Locked files:** never create, modify or delete anything under `tests/contract/`, any `conftest.py`, `pyproject.toml` or `uv.lock`. Add no dependency.
2. **Off limits:** don't edit `SPEC.md`, `docs/`, `evidence/`, `briefs/`, `NOTICE`, `finetune/`, the catalogue's data file or any script. Don't open anything under `evidence/` or `finetune/data*`.
3. **Behaviour that stays:** every earlier contract, and every label and name as written: reconciling never changes a claim or a pack.
4. **No outside contact:** no network, no API keys, no `modal` commands, no git commits.
5. **Tools:** run them as `.venv/bin/ruff` and `.venv/bin/python -m pytest`. One test needs a local network socket that your sandbox forbids; if it is the only failure, say so and close your goal.
