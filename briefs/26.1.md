# The Fireworks label reader asks for low reasoning effort, as Spark 1.3's reader does (SPEC §4)

You are the builder for batch 26.1 of Anamnesis. Read SPEC.md §4, "Reasoning effort for the open-weight reader (batch 26.1)", first: it is the specification. The contract tests are `tests/contract/test_b261_reasoning.py`.

## Why
- **Fireworks timed out.** On 30 Oct, Muse Spark Open on Fireworks timed out on 16 of 18 label reads in a live run.
- **The cause:** `OpenWeightLabelReader` sends no reasoning effort, so Spark Open reasons at its default, `xhigh`. `MetaLabelReader` (`anamnesis/providers/meta.py`:296) asks Spark 1.3 for `"low"`.
- **The test:** at `low`, three reads on Fireworks took 16–24 s, and all three were correct.

## Build
1. **The option** (`anamnesis/providers/openweight.py`). `OpenWeightLabelReader.__init__` gains a keyword `reasoning_effort: str | None = None`, stored as `self.reasoning_effort`.
   - It accepts None or exactly one of `"low"`, `"medium"`, `"high"`, `"xhigh"`. Anything else, including other cases, empty strings and non-strings, raises `ValueError` before any call.
   - When it isn't None, `read()` adds `"reasoning_effort": <value>` to the request body. Nothing else in the body, the estimate, the retry or the length-stop handling changes.
2. **The live script** (`scripts/intake_live.py`). In `intake()`, the open-weight reader gets `reasoning_effort="low"` when `run.open_client.provider == "fireworks"`, and None for Baseten. Update the docstring's `--reader fireworks` sentence to say it asks for low reasoning effort.
3. **Your own tests** go in `tests/unit/test_b261_extra.py`.
