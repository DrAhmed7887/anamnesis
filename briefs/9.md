# Live claim fixes: brand-named intakes and joined brand names

You are the builder for batch 9 of Anamnesis (Python 3.12, uv). In the first live run of claim extraction, two things went wrong:
1. Muse Spark put the **brand** into `intake_amount.inn` (`"concor"`). `_intake_type` in `anamnesis/core/reconcile.py` compared that with the pack's `"bisoprolol"` and returned a false `strength_mismatch`.
2. Voice Transcribe wrote the brand as one word (`"BelocZok"`), which `Catalogue.resolve` could not match against `"Beloc-Zok"`.

## Goal
Make `tests/contract/test_b9_live_claims.py` pass, keeping all other tests green. The fixtures are **real** outputs from 27 Oct.

## Changes
1. **`anamnesis/core/catalogue.py`:** a brand whose normalised form has spaces (from `-`, `/` or whitespace) also matches its **joined** form, as a whole word: `"beloc zok"` also matches `"beloczok"`. Whole-word boundaries still apply, so `"beloczokx"`, `"Belo"` and `"Zok"` don't match.
2. **`anamnesis/core/reconcile.py`:** resolve the intake's ingredient before comparing it.
   - If `canonical_inn(intake.inn)` isn't the pack's ingredient, resolve `intake.inn` through the catalogue. A single-INN entry gives that INN.
   - If the resolved intake ingredient still differs from the pack's ingredient, or can't be resolved, that is an extraction conflict inside one claim → `insufficient_information`, **not** `strength_mismatch`. The claim's identity already matched the pack at rule 4.
3. Update `SPEC.md` §3 rule 7 in one line to describe this. Add your own unit tests in `tests/unit/test_live_claims_extra.py`.
