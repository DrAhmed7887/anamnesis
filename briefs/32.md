# Spanish speech in catalogue resolution (SPEC §3, batch 32)

You are the builder for batch 32 of Anamnesis. Read SPEC.md §3, "Catalogue resolution", with its new bullet "Spanish speech (batch 32)": it is the specification. The contract tests are `tests/contract/test_b32_spanish_names.py`.

## Why
In the Spanish hero's fourth set, a natural Spanish voice said "Tomo bisoprolol". Voice Transcribe wrote "visoprolol" (Spanish says b and v alike) or joined the verb to the name ("Tomobisoprolol", "Tomovisoprolol"). The catalogue matches names as written, so the claim stayed unresolved in 8 of 10 runs and the 10 mg against the 5 mg box was never flagged. The fix is in matching, and only there.

## Build
1. **In `anamnesis/core/catalogue.py`, `Catalogue._matches`:** for Latin-script names only,
   - let each `b` or `v` of a catalogue name match either letter;
   - accept exactly one of `tomo`, `toma`, `tomas`, `tomamos` or `toman` joined directly before the name, inside the match's span, the way Arabic names accept `ARABIC_CLITICS`.

   Keep the whole-word boundaries, and leave Arabic names exactly as they are. Everything built on `_matches` (`resolve`, `covers`, `is_ambiguous`, `matches_brand`, `mentions_moiety`, `brand_entry`) should then follow without changes of its own; check that it does.
2. **Your own tests** go in `tests/unit/test_b32_extra.py`.
