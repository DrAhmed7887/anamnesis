## Changes
1. **C1, units per intake.** When a claim has no `intake_amount` and `regimen.units_per_intake` = u with u ≠ 1, apply rule 8's mapping with r = u against one pack unit: `per` not tablet or capsule → `insufficient_information`; u in {2, 3, 4} → `intake_differs_from_unit_strength`; u = 0.5 → by the pack's splittability (true → `intake_differs_from_unit_strength`; false, or a capsule → `strength_mismatch`; unknown → `insufficient_information`); any other u → `strength_mismatch`. It runs after rule 7 found equal strengths, or when the claim has no strength. It never returns `match` and doesn't count as a comparison for rule 9. Reuse the existing ratio logic rather than copying it.
2. **C1, parser.** In `parse_claims_json`, a `regimen.units_per_intake` that the reply sent but that is dropped (it doesn't parse) also nulls `strength` and `intake_amount`, like the existing dropped-amount rule.
3. **C4.** A `name_text` or `label_text` that names 2+ different catalogue products is an extraction conflict on that side (rule 3, `insufficient_information`), even when the side's identity comes from its strength. A label naming one product by its brand and its ingredient is not a conflict.
4. **C8.** A claim's explicit `strength.release` counts only if `name_text` contains one of SPEC's curated release words (whole word after `catalogue.normalise`) or matches a catalogue brand. Otherwise the claim's release is the brand-implied one, usually unknown. The pack side is unchanged.
5. **Match wording.** The `match` note becomes exactly: `The compared identity and strength details agree.`
