# The clinician brief: one static HTML page per intake, observations across medicines, coverage, and the reply schema in Spark estimates (SPEC §7, §3 pairing, §5)

You are the builder for batch 19 of Anamnesis (Python 3.12, uv), a pre-visit medication-intake tool that reports where a patient's spoken account and the photos of their medicine packs disagree, and never gives advice. Keep the batch focused: no refactors outside what this brief names.

## Goal
Make `tests/contract/test_b19_brief.py` and `tests/contract/test_b19_estimates.py` pass and keep every other test green. Read SPEC.md first: **§7 Clinician brief** (all of it), the §3 "Pairing" bullet that starts "Within a score", and the §5 paragraph "Spark estimates and length stops". Today the intake ends in a `ReconciliationReport` that only a developer can read. After this batch a clinician gets one self-contained HTML page: disagreements first, both sources side by side, every fact linked to the audio turn and the image region it came from.

## Changes
1. **Estimate.** `spark_call_estimate(body)` also counts `len(json.dumps(body["response_format"]))` characters when the body has a `response_format` (default `json.dumps` settings). Nothing else changes: label reads keep `max_tokens: 4000` and claim extraction keeps 16000.
2. **Catalogue.** Append to `catalogue.yaml`: brand `Gelocatil`, aliases `[جيلوكاتيل, جلوكاتيل]`, release `immediate`, splittable `null`, inns `[paracetamol]`, countries `[ES]`, synthetic `true`.
3. **Pairing preference.** In `reconcile_all`, sort candidates by score, then put a pair whose claim `name_text` and pack `label_text` (with `pack.country`) both match the **same catalogue brand** first, then `claim_id`, then `observation_id`. The catalogue already has the lookup you need (`Catalogue._brand_entry`: the resolved entry, only when the text matched that entry's brand or alias name); you may add a public `brand_entry` that returns it.
4. **`anamnesis/core/overlap.py`.**
   - `class OverlapKind(StrEnum)` with `SAME_INGREDIENT = "same_ingredient"` and `SAME_CLASS = "same_class"`.
   - `Overlap`, a frozen dataclass with `kind`, `key`, `claim_ids: tuple[str, ...]`, `audio_evidence: tuple[str, ...]` and `note`.
   - `DRUG_CLASSES = {"beta-blockers": frozenset({"bisoprolol", "metoprolol"})}`, nothing more.
   - `cross_medication(claims, catalogue) -> list[Overlap]`, exactly as SPEC §7 "Cross-medication observations" says. A claim's identity set is the one the reconciler builds (`_claim_identity` in `reconcile.py`, first element; `None` means skip the claim). Its product is the brand of that brand lookup, otherwise `normalise(name_text)`.
5. **Fixed text in `templates.py`:** the three scope lines, the two overlap notes, the coverage line formats, the section headings, "None.", "Unnamed pack", "Not mentioned.", "Not photographed.", the three A3 labels, and the field labels the brief prints. Use the exact strings from SPEC §7. Every one must pass `assert_no_advice`, so avoid words like "take", "stop taking", "continue", "safe" and "skip".
6. **`anamnesis/brief.py`.**
   - `coverage_lines(report, images, audio) -> list[str]` follows SPEC §7 "Coverage", line for line.
   - `render_brief(report, images, audio, catalogue) -> str` returns the page SPEC §7 describes. Prepare each photo as the loop does: `prepare_image`, then open it with PIL to get its size; a `ValueError` or `OSError` means it could not be opened and it gets no figure.
   - The parts the contract checks:
     - the `<header id="scope">`;
     - the 8 `<section id>`s, in order;
     - `<article id="finding:…" data-type="…">` with an `<h3>` heading and `data-side="patient"` / `data-side="pack"` blocks;
     - the overlap `<article data-kind>`s in `across`;
     - `<li>` coverage lines;
     - `<figure id="photo:<i>">` with one `<img>` and an `<svg viewBox>` holding `<a href="#region:…"><rect …/></a>`;
     - `<figure id="region:…">` with the crop;
     - one `<audio src="data:…">`, and `<li id="turn:…" data-start-ms data-end-ms>` turns;
     - exactly one inline `<script>` (clicking a turn plays from its start to its end) and no `on…` attributes;
     - every `href` is a `#` link to an element on the page.
   - **Escape every source string** (transcripts, `name_text`, label text, every model-read value) with `html.escape(..., quote=True)`, in text and in attributes alike. Only fixed text from `templates.py` and numbers go in unescaped.
   - Amounts print as plain decimals with their unit ("100 mg").
   - Style: one small inline `<style>`, plain and print-friendly, no web fonts. Two columns for the two sides on a wide screen and one column below about 640 px. The SVG sits exactly over the photo and scales with it. Label text keeps its line breaks.
   - The output is deterministic: no timestamps, random ids or dict-order surprises.
   - Don't change `run_intake` or the report schema. The brief is computed from a finished report.
7. **Unit tests.** Put your own in `tests/unit/test_brief_extra.py`. Keep each new file under about 400 lines; split `brief.py` into a small helper module if it grows past that.
