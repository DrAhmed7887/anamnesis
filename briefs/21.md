## Changes
1. **Exactly three digit sets.** Today `_AMOUNT_RE` uses `\d`, which in Python matches every Unicode decimal digit. So `"５"` (fullwidth) and `"५"` (Devanagari) parse as 5. Meanwhile `_DIGIT_RUN` in `stated.py` knows only ASCII and Arabic-Indic digits, so a dose said in extended Arabic-Indic digits (`"كونكور ۵ ملغ"`) is never stated and gets dropped. Fix both so they agree:
   - In `_AMOUNT_RE` and in `_DIGIT_RUN` (its run and its lookbehind), the digits are exactly ASCII `0-9`, Arabic-Indic `٠-٩` (U+0660–U+0669) and extended Arabic-Indic `۰-۹` (U+06F0–U+06F9).
   - `٫` keeps working as a dot. `"۲٫۵"` is 2.5, and `"۱٫۰۰۰"` stays ambiguous and returns None.
2. **The Arabic thousands sign `٬` joins a run like a comma.** In `_DIGIT_RUN`, allow `٬` wherever `.` and `,` are allowed: as the one separator inside a run, and in the lookbehind. `parse_amount_text` doesn't accept `٬`, so `"١٬٠٠٠"` and `"٢٬٥"` state nothing, the same way `"1,000"` does. Today they state 1, or 2 and 5. In `tests/unit/test_stated_extra.py`, change the case `("١٬٠٠٠", {"1"})` to `("١٬٠٠٠", set())`: the contract now pins the new behaviour.
3. **The formulation question.** `PATIENT_QUESTIONS["formulation"]` becomes exactly "What salt or release form is shown on your medicine pack?" Since batch 19.1, `formulation_mismatch` also covers salt-only mismatches, so the question must name both causes.
4. **Pins, no change expected.** Two tests guard what the brief already does: a salt named only in `name_text` or the label text shows on both sides, and the Ingredient rows are HTML-escaped with `dir="auto"`. Keep them passing.

## Part 2: fixes found while staging the real-media cases
Contract: `tests/contract/test_b21_media_fixes.py`. SPEC: the new bullet in §2, "A second hundreds group starts a new number", and the new sentence in §7 on `<bdi dir="ltr">`.

5. **A second hundreds group starts a new number** (`anamnesis/providers/stated.py`, `_word_amounts`). Today a later "hundred" multiplies everything before it, so "eight hundred seventy-five and one hundred twenty-five" states 87625 and never 875 or 125. A correctly spoken combination dose is then dropped.
   - Fix: when a hundred word comes and the run's value since its last thousand is already 100 or more, close the earlier number.
   - The plain words after the last `and` go with the new number. In Arabic, a `و` attached to a number word works like `and`.
   - English: "… and one hundred twenty-five" gives 125. Arabic: "… ومية خمسة وعشرين" gives 125.
   - Everything that is one number today stays one number: "two hundred and fifty", "two thousand five hundred and fifty", "ميتين وخمسين", "ألف وخمسمية".
6. **Amounts read left to right on an Arabic row** (`anamnesis/brief_html.py`, `_ingredient_html`). Wrap each amount and its unit in `<bdi dir="ltr">…</bdi>`, so "بيلوك زوك 50 mg" never shows as "mg 50". A combination gets one `<bdi>` per ingredient. Two unit tests in `tests/unit/test_qm_fixes_extra.py` compare the exact strength-row string; update them to the new markup.
