# Durations, "weiß nicht", and a dropped amount's tablet count (SPEC §2, batch 27.1)

You are the builder for batch 27.1 of Anamnesis. Read SPEC.md §2, "Relation checks (batch 27)", and the three places marked "batch 27.1" first: they are the specification. The contract tests are `tests/contract/test_b271_relations.py`; batch 27's contract (`tests/contract/test_b27_relations.py`) still holds.

## Why
Three gaps found after batch 27 merged, two by the Opus supervisor and one live:
- **A duration splits a daily amount:** "I take Concor ten a day for five years" drops the 10, because rule 6 takes "five years" as a splitter.
- **German puts the negation after the word of knowing:** "ich weiß nicht fünf Milligramm" drops the 5.
- **A dropped amount leaves a tablet count behind:** in the live probe, the tuned Glimmer read "ten milligrams a day, five in the morning and five at night" as a 10 mg intake and five tablets. Rule 6 dropped the 10, but the five stayed and gave a false `strength_mismatch`.

## Build
1. **Durations** (`anamnesis/providers/relations.py`): with no unit word after a run, a duration word right after it makes the run a duration, never a mention. A run that ends in "a" is never a duration ("ten a day"). Keep the SPEC's duration words as a module constant with a SPEC reference.
2. **"weiß nicht":** a negation word right after a word of knowing doesn't negate the number.
3. **The tablet count** (the hook in `anamnesis/providers/claims.py`): when the relation checks drop a claim's amount, its `units_per_intake` goes too, unless a count mention (rule 3) in the cited turns has that value. A small function in `relations.py` that returns the values of the runs rule 3 reads as counts will do. The existing pairing rule then applies as usual.
4. **Your own tests** go in `tests/unit/test_b271_extra.py`.
