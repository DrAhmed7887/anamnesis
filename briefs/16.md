You are the builder for batch 16 of Anamnesis (Python 3.12, uv), a pre-visit medication-intake tool that reports where a patient's spoken account and the photos of their medicine packs disagree, and never gives advice. Keep this batch small and surgical: no refactors, nothing outside this brief.

## Goal

## Changes
1. **One shared number parser** for model-written amounts, in a small new module such as `anamnesis/providers/numbers.py`: `"2.5"` and `"2,5"` parse to a `Decimal`; a comma is a decimal mark only when 1 or 2 digits follow it; `"1,000"`, ranges (`"1-2"`), words, empty strings, zero and negatives return `None`.
2. **Optional fields that don't parse become null instead of failing the reply:** `regimen.units_per_intake` and `regimen.intakes_per_day` (each on its own), `intake_amount`, `strength` (null when any ingredient amount doesn't parse, or when the `Strength` model rejects it, for example two salts of one INN), and the label's `country`. Do this before pydantic validation, so one bad field never drops the other claims.
3. **Grounding:** a claim's `name_text` must occur in the transcript of at least one turn the claim cites: `normalise(name_text)` as a substring of `normalise(transcript)`. If not, raise the same retryable `ProviderError("spark", ...)` the parser raises today for an uncited turn.
4. **Country:** keep it only if it is exactly two letters, stored upper-case; anything else becomes null.
5. **Advice:** run `assert_no_advice` over `name_text`, the claim's `inn`, the label's `inn` and every ingredient `inn` (in `strength` and `intake_amount`), on the raw strings before parsing. A hit is a retryable `ProviderError`. `label_text` is exempt: it is quoted source text.
