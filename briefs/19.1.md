## Changes
1. **The Arabic decimal sign.** In `parse_amount_text`, read `٫` (U+066B) as a dot, so every dot rule applies to it unchanged: `"٢٫٥"` is 2.5, `"٠٫١٢٥"` is 0.125, and `"١٫٠٠٠"` stays ambiguous and returns None. Arabic-Indic digits already parse; keep that. The Arabic thousands sign `٬` must not parse.
2. **Arabic advice words with clitics.** `assert_no_advice` must also catch `يجب`, `خذ`, `تناول`, `توقف`, `ضاعف` and `ينصح` in two cases:
   - with one of the attached clitics `وال`, `بال`, `فال`, `لل`, `ال`, `و`, `ف`, `ب`, `ل` in front, as whole words otherwise;
   - after diacritics and tatweel are removed. `anamnesis.core.catalogue.normalise` already removes them, and it leaves these six words unchanged.

   The English and Spanish/German words keep their current word-boundary matching. Names such as "كونكور ٥ ملغ", "البنادول", "الصيدلية" and "مناولة" must still pass.
3. **Feminine release words.** Add the feminine `ممتدة` and `بطيئة` to the Arabic release words. The check runs on `normalise(name_text)`, which writes `ة` as `ه`, so add them in that spelling, `ممتده` and `بطيئه`, to both `_RELEASE_WORDS` and `_ARABIC_RELEASE_WORDS`. The clitics then apply to them as to the masculine forms.
4. **The formulation note.** `TEMPLATES[D.FORMULATION_MISMATCH]` becomes exactly "Reported and pack formulations differ (salt or release form)."
5. **A fourth scope line**, after the other three: exactly "Checks across medicines cover shared ingredients and one curated class (beta-blockers); interactions were not assessed."
6. **No overlap from a conflicted claim.** In `overlap.py`, a claim counts only when `_claim_identity` gives an identity set **and** no extraction conflict (its second value is False).
7. **Salts on both sides.** In the brief's strength rows (patient and pack) and the patient's intake row, show each ingredient's `salt` after its INN when there is one, for example "metoprolol tartrate 100 mg". A salt-only `formulation_mismatch` must then show "tartrate" on the patient side and "succinate" on the pack side. Escape the salt like every other source string.
8. **A region whose photo is missing is refused.** In `_photos_html`, a report region whose photo isn't among the prepared photos raises `ValueError` instead of being skipped (`continue`).
9. **Leftovers.**
   - The `reconcile_all` docstring says pairs are taken "by score". They are now taken by same brand first, then score, then ids.
   - Delete the unused `FIELD_EVIDENCE` constant.
   - Leave the turn script as it is: the contract now runs it in Node, and it passes.
