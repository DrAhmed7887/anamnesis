# Spanish salt names (SPEC §3, batch 34)

You are the builder for batch 34 of Anamnesis. Read SPEC.md §3, "Decision order for one claim and one pack", rule 6, with its new bullet "Spanish salt names (batch 34)" and the bullets under it: they are the specification. The contract tests are `tests/contract/test_b34_spanish_salts.py`.

## Why
Every salt the product reads comes from `INN_ALIASES` (`anamnesis/schema/medication.py`), whose salt names are English or Latin. A Spanish salt name was read as no salt at all, so "metoprolol succinato" against a Metoprolol tartrate pack, or a claim named "Metoprolol succinate" against Spain's Lopresor box ("Metoprolol, tartrato"), was a `match`. That hides a formulation difference from the clinician. The product's Spanish test runs use Spain's registry box images, which print the salt in Spanish.

## Build
1. **The table:** in `anamnesis/schema/medication.py`, beside `INN_ALIASES`, a dict of exactly the Spanish words the SPEC bullet lists, each mapped to its English salt. Export it from `anamnesis.schema` as `INN_ALIASES` is.
2. **The reading:** in `anamnesis/core/reconcile.py`, `_loose_salts` reads a curated (moiety, salt) pair when the salt's English name or one of its Spanish words stands as whole words in the normalised text, with the existing moiety-or-brand check. A Spanish word yields its English salt, and the existing suppression of a salt inside a longer one runs on the English names.
3. **Nothing else changes:** `INN_ALIASES`, `canonical_inn`, `salt_of`, the schema's validator, `_nominal_pack_amount`, the scalar `inn`, the claim parser, `catalogue.normalise` and `Catalogue._matches` all stay as they are. Add no word beyond the SPEC's list.
4. **Your own tests** go in `tests/unit/test_b34_extra.py`.

## Hard rules
1. **Locked files:** never create, modify or delete anything under `tests/contract/`, any `conftest.py`, `pyproject.toml` or `uv.lock`. Add no dependency.

3. **Behaviour that stays:** every earlier contract, and every label and name as written: reconciling never changes a claim or a pack.
4. **No outside contact:** no network, no API keys, no `modal` commands, no git commits.
5. **Tools:** run them as `.venv/bin/ruff` and `.venv/bin/python -m pytest`. One test needs a local network socket that your sandbox forbids; if it is the only failure, say so and close your goal.
