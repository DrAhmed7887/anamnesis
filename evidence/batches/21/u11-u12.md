## U12, the hero, 10 runs: 0 of 10 as pre-registered, and no harmful reading
The scorer is `u12_score.py`, in this folder. The files are `report_U12_r01.json` to `report_U12_r10.json`.

| Runs | Concor (10 mg said, 5 mg box) | Beloc-Zok (50 mg said, Bloxazoc 50 mg box) | Beta-blocker note |
|---|---|---|---|
| r02, r03, r04, r06, r07, r08 | `intake_differs_from_unit_strength` | `match` | yes |
| r01, r05, r09 | `insufficient_information` | `insufficient_information` | yes |
| r10 | `intake_differs_from_unit_strength` | `unresolved_name` ("BelocZokZok") | no |

- **The key error:** the row expected `strength_mismatch` for Concor. "I take Concor ten milligrams every morning" states an intake, not the tablet's strength, so the system reports `intake_differs_from_unit_strength`: 10 mg taken against a 5 mg tablet. This is the CEO's error, the same one U7 records, and the row stays as written.
- **Read with the system's own categories (decided after the run, not pre-registered):**
  - the Concor dose was flagged as different from the box in 7 of 10 runs, and failed safe in the other 3;
  - Beloc-Zok matched in 6, and failed safe in 4;
  - the beta-blocker note appeared in 9;
  - all three together came out in 6 of 10 (r02–r04 and r06–r08).
- **Harm check:** no run had a false `match`, or a wrong strength or ingredient shown as fact.
- **Every miss starts on the speech side:**
  - Voice Transcribe returned 7 different transcripts of the same file.
  - In 7 of the 10, a word or number was repeated: "ten ten", "10 10", "ten 10", "BelocZok BelocZok" or "BelocZokZok".
  - In r01, r05 and r09 both claims came back without amounts, and in r10 "BelocZokZok" didn't resolve.
  - The adapter doesn't keep the claim replies, so why r05's digits were dropped can't be told from here.
  - No reading of either box showed a wrong strength.
- **Limit:** this is one synthetic voice and one file, run 10 times. Human speech may transcribe differently.
- **Cost and time:** $0.38766 for the 10 runs, 31.6–39.7 s each. The claim requests took 7.4–11.5 s, inside the 30 s timeout.
